# DO NOT REGENERATE: the committed chapter files were NOT produced by
#   npx tsx scripts/fetch-wikisource.ts --sources data/books-src/kumaravyasa-bharata-virataparva/sources.txt
# and re-running it would overwrite them with worse output. In these pages line 3 of many verses
# ends in a bare double danda with no number, which the shared joinNumberedVerses() treats as a
# verse break (splitting such verses into 3-line halves), and the pages mix ASCII-digit and
# Kannada-digit verse numbers in several danda styles. Instead a one-off script (not committed)
# read the same raw wikitext from the API on 2026-09-24 and, mechanically:
#   - took the poem body (the <poem> block where present, else the text between the page's own
#     heading and its "ನೋಡಿ" trailer), dropping <ref> citations, <big> and bold markers
#   - kept each sandhi's opening ಸೂಚನೆ (the three lines after the "ಸೂ:" / "ಸೂ." / "ಸೂಚನೆ:"
#     prefix) as its own block, headed by a "ಸೂಚನೆ" line, as in the adiparva and sabhaparva books
#   - joined lines into one block per verse, ending a verse only at a line carrying a verse
#     number, and stripped all dandas; verse numbers normalised to a Kannada numeral at the
#     end of line 6
#   - dropped the separator lines of at-signs, stars and guillemets between sandhis and the
#     closing Sanskrit-style colophon after 10.85 (ಇತಿ ಶ್ರೀಮದಚಿಂತ್ಯ ಮಹಿಮ ... ವಿರಾಟಪರ್ವಂ ಸಮಾಪ್ತಮಾದುದು)
#   - NFC; whitespace collapsed
# Checked mechanically after the rewrite: every block is exactly 6 lines (ಸೂಚನೆ 3), and verse
# numbers run 1..N with no gaps or repeats, N = 34, 56, 110, 61, 48, 66, 52, 93, 41, 85 (646).
#
# Source oddities handled by hand:
#   - sandhi 1 gives verse 11 twice, byte-identical apart from a danda; the second copy dropped

#     the index and its content are sandhi 3, and the chapter is titled ಮೂರನೆಯ ಸಂಧಿ
#   - chapter titles are the ordinal names from the Wikisource page titles; the sandhis
#     give themselves no other names
