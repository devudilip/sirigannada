# Chikkalluru Siddappaji Jatre and the Manteswamy epic (ಚಿಕ್ಕಲ್ಲೂರು ಸಿದ್ದಪ್ಪಾಜಿ ಜಾತ್ರೆ, ಮಂಟೇಸ್ವಾಮಿ ಕಾವ್ಯ): research brief (verified 2026-11-24)

## Read first

- **Solid:**
  - Place: Chikkalluru is a forest-edge kshetra in **Kollegal taluk**, Chamarajanagar district, inside the **Hanur assembly constituency** (Oneindia 2024). That is why some reports carry a "ಹನೂರು" dateline. The 2026 reports are datelined Kollegal and quote the Kollegal tahsildar, so do not move it to Hanur taluk. The village is in Tellanur gram panchayat. It is about 20 km from Kollegal (Hiriyanna) or 27 km from the taluk centre (temple blog). The Kaveri and Muttatti are close by.
  - 2026 jatre: **3–7 Jan 2026**, five days and five nights:
    - 3 Jan: ಚಂದ್ರಮಂಡಲೋತ್ಸವ, at night.
    - 4 Jan: ಹುಲಿವಾಹನೋತ್ಸವ and ದೊಡ್ಡವರ ಸೇವೆ.
    - 5 Jan: ರುದ್ರಾಕ್ಷಿ ಮಂಟಪೋತ್ಸವ, with ಮುಡಿಸೇವೆ and ನೀಲಗಾರ ದೀಕ್ಷೆ.
    - 6 Jan: ಗಜವಾಹನೋತ್ಸವ and ಪಂಕ್ತಿ ಸೇವೆ.
    - 7 Jan: ಮುತ್ತುರಾಯರ ಸೇವೆ, also called ಕಡೆ ಬಾಗಿಲು ಸೇವೆ.
    - Sources: Prajavani 30 Dec 2025, Janamana 3 Jan 2026, Andolana 3 Jan 2026.
  - Earlier start dates: 2021 Jan 28; 2024 Jan 25; 2025 Jan 13. The date moves with the full moon.
  - The Chandramandala:
    - It is a bamboo frame, shaped like a ಕಂಡಾಯ or a chariot. Torches are wound on every node and the frame is smeared with ghee that devotees vowed.
    - Materials come from the surrounding villages: bamboo from Tellanur, oil and torches from Maskayyana Doddi, madi cloth from Banur and Balagunase.
    - The Gurumane Neelagaras build it.
    - The swamy of the Boppegowdanapura math lights it. In 2013, 2021 and 2026 this was Jnanananda Chennaraje Urs.
    - It is lit lying down. Once it catches, the crowd lifts it upright with a shout of "ಉಘೇ".
    - People throw grain, fruit and coins into the fire. The direction the flame leans tells where the rains and crops will be good. The black soot is carried home as protection.
    - Sources: Prajavani 2025-12-30; Hiriyanna pp. ೨೪೦–೨೪೧; DH 2013; TV9 2021.
  - The epic gives a strong, specific story for Siddappaji (ಕೆಂಪಣ್ಣ / ಕೆಂಪಾಚಾರಿ). The episodes are in a published, field-recorded text (Hiriyanna, Karnataka Janapada Vishwavidyalaya; full Kannada text on archive.org):
    - He is the poor smith family's lucky 7th son.
    - Muddamma refuses to give her youngest son to the begging jangama: "ನನ್ನ ಕಿರಿಮಗನ ಕೊಡೋದಿಲ್ಲ".
    - The seven-year-old Kempanna answers with a riddle at the forge and drops a red-hot ember on Manteswamy's palm. The ember becomes a lotus.
    - Twelve years shut in the Kalinga cave on Kunduru hill, riding a tiger round the hill.
    - The iron-alms mission to the Panchalas of Halaguru–Chillapura, disguised as a bangle-seller, and the trials there, ending with sitting on the molten ಕಾದಗಟ್ಟಿ.
    - Manteswamy's command: "go to Chikkalluru and become one" (ಐಕ್ಯ).
  - The epic itself has Siddappaji lay out the jatre days: Chandramandala on the full-moon night, then ದೊಡ್ಡೋರ ಸೇವೆ, ಮುಡಿ ಸೇವೆ, and so on. It also has him promise a ಮುತ್ತತ್ತಿರಾಯ ಸೇವೆ at Chikkalluru.
  - Law, in sequence, from Deccan Herald:
    - Police awareness drive: 2015.
    - HC order of 20 Jan 2016, on the Vishwa Prani Kalyana Mandali petition.
    - Seizures in 2016: 82 goats, 8 sheep, 50 country chickens and 1,800 broilers at 16 checkposts, plus five arrests.
    - HC interim order allowing Panktiseve: 13 Jan 2017.
    - DC ban for 2–7 Jan 2018.
    - Devotees bring cooked or raw meat from home: 2018 and 2019.
    - Covid cancellation: 2021.
    - Oneindia 2023: thousands still cut sheep and fowl away from the temple.
    - DC order with 7 checkposts: 2024.
    - Minister H.C. Mahadevappa, 2026: there is "no bali peetha" and food is a right.
- **Disputed (details in Disputes):**
  - **Siddappaji's parents and earlier birth.** One version has Bachi Basavachari and Muddamma at Nidughatta/Maralli, and makes him the rebirth of the Kalyana gate-guard Katukara Sangayya. A second (Shastriya Kannada) makes him the rebirth of Baachi Basavayya himself. Others give "Muddoji" (DH 2019; temple blog: "ಮುದ್ದೋಜಿ ಮತ್ತು ಲಿಂಗಮ್ಮ").
  - Where the cow-kicks-the-pail scene (the ಮಂಟೇಸ್ವಾಮಿ naming) happens: Maralli or Adihonnayakanahalli.
  - Moon: ಪುಷ್ಯ ಹುಣ್ಣಿಮೆ (Hiriyanna, OCR "ಪುಷ್ಕಪೂರ್ಣಿಮೆ") or "full moon of Maga masa" (DH 2013).
  - Who builds the Chandramandala: the Gurumane Neelagaras (Prajavani), the seven villages (Hiriyanna), or "only the ಆದಿಜಾಂಬವ community" (TV9 2025).
  - Day-by-day naming differs between sources.
  - Manteswamy's date: c. 15th century, or 15th–16th century. The epic's identification with 12th-century Allama Prabhu "does not find many takers". Hiriyanna's notes also carry "ಚಾಮರಾಜ ಒಡೆಯರ (ಕ್ರಿಶ.೧೩೬೦)".
  - Length of a full recital: 3–5 nights, or 10 days and 10 nights.
  - Whether animal sacrifice was ever "custom". Researcher Mahadeva Shankanapura says no; the animal-welfare petitioner says cooking meat on site equals sacrifice.

## B. Siddappaji in the tradition (all `legend` unless marked)

> Main text for B is the field-recorded Manteswamy epic printed in Hiriyanna (fact 2). The narrative chapters are headed "ಶಿಸುಮಗನಾದ ಸಿದ್ಧಪ್ಪಾಜಿಯನ್ನು ದಾನಪಡೆಯುವುದು - ೧೩" and "ಸಿದ್ದಪ್ಪಾಜಿ ಪವಾಡ - ೧೭". The editor's variant-notes, pp. ೨೨೮–೨೪೦, compare texts labelled ಅ, ಇ, ಉ… The book names its singers (fact 43). The full-story recording was by Purigali Mahadevu and Bannur Kempamma.

9. **His earlier life: the Kalyana gate-guard Katukara Sangayya begged Manteswamy to be born again as a better man. At the same moment, at Nidughatta, the starving Muddamma begged for food. The rebirth is Kempanna of Nidughatta. "Generally all texts" tell this.**
   - Tier: legend
   - Source: Hiriyanna, variant-notes §28, p. ೨೨೯ (URL as fact 2)
   - Quote: "ಈ ಮರುಜನ್ನವೇ ಕೆಂಪಣ್ಣನಾಗಿ ನಿಡುಘಟ್ಟದಲ್ಲಿ ಅವತರಿಸುತ್ತಾರೆ ಎಂಬ ಭಾಗವನ್ನು ಸಾಮಾನ್ಯವಾಗಿ ಎಲ್ಲಾ ಪಠ್ಯಗಳು ತಿಳಿಸುತ್ತವೆ."
   - "ಮರುಜನ್ನವೇ" is the OCR's reading of ಮರುಜನ್ಮವೇ.
   - Variant (Shastriya Kannada, fact 25): the one reborn is **Baachi Basavayya**, "reborn as Baala Kempanna and later acquires the name Sidapaaji." See Disputes.

10. **Birth: his earlier name was Kempachari or Kempanna. He was born at Nidughatta, about 4 km from Malavalli, the 7th son of Bachibasavachari and Muddamma (text ಪ). The family left Nidughatta in poverty for Maralli, and after the 7th son came, "poverty left, wealth came".**
    - Tier: legend
    - Source: Hiriyanna, variant-notes §65, p. ೨೩೯ and §32 (URL as fact 2)
    - Quote: "ಸಿದ್ದಪ್ಪಾಜಿಯವರ ಪೂರ್ವ ನಾಮಧೇಯ ಕೆಂಪಾಚಾರಿ ಅಥವಾ ಕೆಂಪಣ್ಣ."
    - Epic text, p. ೭೮: "ಮಾರಳ್ಳಿ ಒಳಗೆ ಕಿರಿಮಗ ಕೆಂಪಾಚಾರಿ ಹುಟ್ಟಿದ ಮೇಲೆ ದರಿದ್ರ ದೂರವಾಯ್ತು"
    - The epic adds that he was born after "ಒಂಬತ್ತು ಹಗಲು ಒಂಬತ್ತು ರಾತ್ರಿಗೆ ನೆತ್ತಿ ಮೇಲೆ ಕುರುಜಡೆ ಸಹಿತವಾಗಿ" (p. ೭೭), with matted locks already on his head.
    - Text ತ puts the birth at Adihonnayakanahalli instead.

11. **Muddamma gave each of her seven sons a trade: gold, silver, copper, wood, coral, pearl. The youngest, Kempachari, got iron.**
    - Tier: legend
    - Source: Hiriyanna, epic text p. ೭೯ (URL as fact 2)
    - Quote: "ಕೆಂಪಾಚಾರಿಗೆ ಕಬ್ಬಿಣದ ಉದ್ಯೋಗ"
    - The house is described as having gold on the winnowing tray, coral walls and a pearl door ("ಮೊರದಲ್ಲಿ ಬಂಗಾರ ಹವಳದಲ್ಲಿ ಗೋಡೆ"). Good image material.

12. **Episode, "the talking pearl": Manteswamy comes to Muddamma's door with his ಕಂಡಾಯ and ತಂಬೂರಿ while she is milking. She offers pearls and gold, "three measures each". He refuses them and asks for the child. She refuses in a long refrain and slams the seven doors.**
    - Tier: legend
    - Source: Hiriyanna, epic text pp. ೮೪–೮೬ (URL as fact 2)
    - Quote: "ಮುತ್ತು ನಿಮ್ಮ ಮನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಇರುವ ಕಂದನನ್ನು ನನಗೆ ದಾನವಾಗಿ ಕೊಡವ್ವ"
    - Her answer: "ಹೆತ್ತ ಮಕ್ಕಳನ್ನು ಬಿತ್ತಿದ ಬೆಳೆಯನ್ನು ಯಾರು ದಾನ ಕೊಡುತ್ತಾರೆ."
    - Refrain: "ನನ್ನ ಕಿರಿಮಗನ ಕೊಡೋದಿಲ್ಲ"
    - His promise, in the same scene: "ನಾನು ಒರಗೋದೇವರು ಆಗುತ್ತೀನಿ ಅವ್ನ ಮೆರೆಯೋ ದೇವರು ಮಾಡುತ್ತೀನಿ ಕಂದ"

13. **How "Mante" got his name: frightened by the light of the ಕಂಡಾಯ and the tamburi's sound, the cow kicked over the milk pot. Muddamma cursed the jangama with "ಮಂಟುಕೋ", meaning roughly "fall over / topple", and he smiled and took the name.**
    - Tier: legend
    - Source: Hiriyanna, variant-notes §33, p. ೨೨೯–೨೩೦ (URL as fact 2)
    - Quote: "ಹಸು ಬೆದರಲು ಕಾರಣನಾದ ಜಂಗಮನಿಗೆ ಮಂಟುಕೊ ಎಂಬ ಮಾತನ್ನು ಮುದ್ದಮ್ಮ ಆಡಿದುದೇ ಹೊಸ ನಾಮಕರಣಕ್ಕೆ ಕಾರಣವಾಯಿತು."
    - Variant: texts ಟ, ತ, ಪ place the scene not at Maralli but at Adihonnayakanahalli, with "ಬಳೆಮುದ್ದಮ್ಮ" (§34). See Disputes.

14. **Manteswamy's anger: he sits on a platform under an ashwattha tree, smokes huge amounts of bhangi in a gold chillum, blows the smoke over Maralli, and calls up 7771 ಮಾರಿ goddesses to ruin Muddamma's house.**
    - Tier: legend
    - Source: Hiriyanna, epic text pp. ೮೬–೮೯ (URL as fact 2)
    - Quote: "ಗ್ಯಾನದಲ್ಲಿ ೭೭೭೧ ಜನ ಮಾರಿಯರನ್ನು ಕೂಗುತ್ತಾರೆ."
    - Writer's note: ಭಂಗಿ (cannabis) runs through the epic and the cult (see fact 50). Decide how to handle it for a children's book.

15. **Episode, "the riddle and the ember": Manteswamy comes to the forge and asks for one live coal for his chillum. Kempanna, aged seven, mocks him with a riddle about the goldsmith who makes the tali, then picks a coal "the size of a wood-apple" with iron tongs and drops it on Manteswamy's open palm. The fire-god trembles and sits there as a lotus.**
    - Tier: legend
    - Source: Hiriyanna, epic text pp. ೯೬–೯೮; variant-notes §42 (URL as fact 2)
    - Quote: "ತಾಳಿ ಮಾಡಿದ ಗಂಡ ನಾನು ತಾಳಿ ಕಟ್ಟದ ಗಂಡ ನೀನು ಕಾಣೋ"
    - Second quote: "ಬೇಲದ ಕಾಯಿಗಾತ್ರದಲ್ಲಿರುವ ಬೆಂಕಿಕೆಂಡವನ್ನು ತೆಗೆದು ಸ್ವಾಮಿಯವರ ಅಂಗೈ ಮೇಲೆ ಇಟ್ಟಾಗ"
    - Variant-notes, texts ಅ, ಇ, ಉ, ಎ, ಐ: Manteswamy fills the coals into his chillum, takes three puffs, and gives Kempanna ನೀಲಿ ಗ್ಯಾನ. Kempanna is sent "round the Chandramandala and Suryamandala" and then shut in the Kalinga cave for 12 years.

16. **Prophecy, inside the ember scene: Manteswamy names the future Chikkalluru.**
    - Manteswamy says he will make the boy a lone god under a lone tamarind tree at Chikkalluru, among Iradalu, Balagunase, Kottanur, Tellanur, Banur, Sundralli and Ankanapura.
    - He curses the tamarind never to flower or fruit, "as you will have no worldly pleasure".
    - Tier: legend
    - Source: Hiriyanna, epic text p. ೯೭ (URL as fact 2)
    - Quote: "ನಾಳೆ ಚಿಕ್ಕಲ್ಲೂರು ಮಾಡಿ ಆ ಒಂಟಿ ಹುಣಸೆಮರದಡಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಒಂಟಿ ದೇವರು ಮಾಡುತ್ತೀನಿ ಕಂದ."
    - The editor's note (§37) says the Siddappaji ಸಾಲು opens with exactly this description: "ಚಿಕ್ಕೇಗೌಡನದೊಡ್ಡಿಯೇ ಚಿಕ್ಕಲ್ಲೂರು ಕ್ಷೇತ್ರ ಎಂಬುದಾಗಿ ಮುಖ್ಯ ಪಠ್ಯಗಳಲ್ಲಿ ಬರುತ್ತದೆ." The epic's "ಕೊಯಮತ್ತೂರು ಜಿಲ್ಲೆ ಕೊಳ್ಳೇಗಾಲ ತಾಲ್ಲೂಕು" reflects the old Madras Presidency district.

17. **Twelve years in the Kalinga cave and the tiger ride. The seven rishis of the Kalinga cave on Kunduru hill would make room only if he rode their ಕರಿಬರಗ round the hill seven times. He tamed it with three tugs and rode it sixteen times round, and the rishis left the cave to him. During his tapas an anthill grew over his head and bees nested in his nose.**
    - Tier: legend
    - Source: Hiriyanna, variant-notes §67–68, pp. ೨೩೯–೨೪೦ (URL as fact 2)
    - Quote: "ಬರಗ ಎಂದರೆ ಭಯಂಕರವಾದ ಹುಲಿ."
    - Second quote: "ಮೇಲೆ ಕೂತು ಕೆಂಪಣ್ಣ ಹದಿನಾರು ಸುತ್ತು ಬೆಟ್ಟವನ್ನು ಸುತ್ತಿ ಬಂದನು."
    - Also: "ಮೂಗಿನ ರಂಧ್ರದಲ್ಲಿ ಜೇನುಗೂಡನ್ನು ನಿರ್ಮಿಸಿದೆ."
    - "ಈ ಭಾಗ ಎಲ್ಲಾ ಪಠ್ಯಗಳಲ್ಲಿ ಒಂದೇ ರೀತಿಯಾಗಿ ಬರುತ್ತದೆ."
    - No source explicitly links this tiger to the jatre's ಹುಲಿವಾಹನೋತ್ಸವ. Do not assert the link.

18. **Coming out: after 12 years a needle-thin sunbeam came through a chink in the slab. Siddappaji kicked the stone, and it spun like a top and fell in front of the Boppegowdanapura math, where it still gets secret worship three times a day.**
    - Tier: legend
    - Source: Hiriyanna, variant-notes §53, p. ೨೩೬ (URL as fact 2)
    - Quote: "ಬುಗುರಿ ಸುತ್ತುವಂತೆ ಸುತ್ತಿ ಕೆಳಗೆ ಬಿದ್ದಿತಂತೆ."

19. **The first Neelagara initiation. Doddammatayi welcomed all the sharanas. Kempanna was bathed in the tank, given cow urine "for birth-purity", taught the mantra, and had the ನೀಲರುದ್ರಾಕ್ಷಿ tied at his neck by Manteswamy's own hand. Siddappaji is "the first Neelagara".**
    - Tier: legend
    - Source: Hiriyanna, variant-notes §45, p. ೨೩೩; field-work chapter p. ೧೯೫ (URL as fact 2)
    - Quote: "ಕೆಂಪಣ್ಣನಿಗೆ ನೀಲಗಾರ ದೀಕ್ಷೆಯನ್ನು ಕೊಡಿಸಲು ಸಜ್ಜಾದ ಕಾರ್ಯಕ್ರಮಕ್ಕೆ ಎಲ್ಲಾ ಶರಣರುಗಳನ್ನು ದೊಡ್ಡಮ್ಮತಾಯಿ ಬರಮಾಡಿಕೊಳ್ಳುತ್ತಾಳ."
    - Second quote: "ಮೊದಲ ನೀಲಗಾರ ದೀಕ್ಷೆಯನ್ನು ಪಡೆದವನು ಸಿದ್ದಪ್ಪಾಜಿ."
    - Support: M. S. Chandra & Remya V. P., *The Eastern Anthropologist* 75:1-2 (2022) (fact 26 URL): "Siddappaji, the greatest disciple of Manteswamy, who is also the first Neelagara (ghana neeli)". This is the origin of his title ಘನನೀಲಿ.

20. **The mission: iron alms from the Panchalas of Halaguru–Chillapura to build the Boppanapura math. Siddappaji first offers to smash Kunduru hill into heaps of gold. Manteswamy says gold is a cheating Lakshmi and iron is the "first gold", and sends him begging for iron with a promise.**
    - Tier: legend
    - Source: Hiriyanna, epic text pp. ೧೧೮–೧೧೯ (URL as fact 2)
    - Quote: "ಕಬ್ಬಿಣ ಭಿಕ್ಷೆಗೆ ಹಲಗೂರು ಚಿಲ್ಲಾಪುರಕ್ಕೆ ಪ್ರಯಾಣವ"
    - Manteswamy's promise: "ಒರಗೋದೇವರು ನಾನಾಗುತ್ತೀನಿ ಮೆರೆಯುವ ದೇವರನ್ನಾಗಿ ನಿನ್ನ ಮಾಡುತ್ತೀನಿ"
    - Siddappaji's reluctance, since these are his own caste-people whose village he once saw ruined: "ಈಗ ನಾನು ಅವರ ಊರಿಗೆ ಹೋಗಿ ಕಬ್ಬಿಣ ಭಿಕ್ಷೆ ತರಬೇಕೇ"
    - Editor (§32): "ಹಲಗೂರು ಚಿಲ್ಲಾಪುರದಲ್ಲಿ ಕಬ್ಬಿಣ ಭಿಕ್ಷೆಯ ಪವಾಡದ ಭಾಗವನ್ನು ನೀಲಗಾರರು ಅತ್ಯುತ್ಸಾಹದಿಂದ ಹಾಡುತ್ತಾರೆ."

21. **Episode, "the bangle-seller": to get into Halaguru he talks his way past the toll-goddess Sunkada Maramma and walks the queen's street disguised as a bangle-seller, calling the palegars' wives to buy "Bombay bangles".**
    - Tier: legend
    - Source: Hiriyanna, epic text pp. ೧೬೧–೧೬೨ (URL as fact 2)
    - Quote: "ಸ್ವಾಮಿಯವರು ಬಳೆಗಾರ ಶೆಟ್ರ ವೇಷವನ್ನು ತಾಳ್ಕೊಂಡು"
    - Second source: DH 2019 (fact 4): "On an occasion, Siddappaji entered the guarded magnetic fort at Halaguru as a bangle-seller."

22. **The Halaguru trials (text ಚ):**
    - He swallows iron filings.
    - He flies a steel horse into the sky.
    - He drinks a cauldron of cactus milk.
    - He survives a pit filled with chillies and cactus milk under a stone slab.
    - He wears red-hot iron bangles and beams.
    - He sits on the blazing molten ingot (ಕಾದಗಟ್ಟಿ).
    - Tier: legend
    - Source: Hiriyanna, variant-notes §66, p. ೨೩೯ (URL as fact 2)
    - Quote: "ಉಕ್ಕಿನ ಕುದುರೆಯನ್ನು ಗಗನಕ್ಕೆ ಹಾರಿಸಿದ್ದು"
    - Also: "ಪ್ರಖರ ಜ್ವಾಲೆಯಿಂದ ಕೂಡಿದ ಕಾದಗಟ್ಟಿಯ ಮೇಲೆ ಕುಳಿತದ್ದು."
    - Pit episode in the epic text, p. ೧೭೯: "ಮೂರು ದಿನ ಮಧ್ಯಾನ ಕಳೆದ ಮೇಲೆ ಮುಚ್ಚಿದ ಚಪ್ಪಡಿ ಕಲ್ಲು ಕಿತ್ತಾಗ". He climbs out carrying the ಕಂಡಾಯ.

23. **Episode, "the molten ingot" (ಕಾದಗಟ್ಟಿ), the climax:**
    - The Panchalas melt iron, panchaloha and silver into an ingot "as big as 12 huttis".
    - Siddappaji stands weeping before it and calls out to Boppanapura.
    - Doddamma hears "her son's cry" and makes Manteswamy, Rachappaji and Channajamma come and give him darshana.
    - The women of the smiths' street gather with babies on their hips "to see how he dies".
    - He appeals to the mothers as their own child.
    - Tier: legend
    - Source: Hiriyanna, epic text pp. ೧೭೯–೧೮೦ (URL as fact 2)
    - Quote: "ಸಿದ್ದಪ್ಪಾಜಿ ಕಾದಗಟ್ಟಿಯ ಮುಂದೆ ಅಳುತ್ತಾ ನಿಂತಿರುವರಂತೆ."
    - Second quote: "ಮಗ ಕೂಗಿದ ಕೂಗು ದೊಡ್ಡಮ್ಮ ತಾಯಿ ಪಾದಕ್ಕೆ ಪ್ರಿಯವಾಯಂತೆ."
    - His appeal: "ಮಕ್ಕಳ ಹೆತ್ತಿರುವ ತಂದೆ ತಾಯಿಗಳೇ"

24. **The ending: aikya at Chikkalluru. After the Panchalas built the Boppegowdanapura math ("in all texts", §61), Manteswamy cursed the Halaguru people and told Siddappaji to go and become one with Chikkalluru, with a lavish jatre in his honour. Manteswamy himself withdrew underground at Boppegowdanapura behind a great boulder "until Kali ends".**
    - Tier: legend
    - Source: Hiriyanna, epic text (end of Siddappaji cycle) (URL as fact 2)
    - Quote: "ನೀನು ಚಿಕ್ಕಳ್ಳಿ ಗ್ರಾಮ ಚಿಕ್ಕಲ್ಲೂರು ಕ್ಷೇತ್ರಕ್ಕೆ ಹೋಗಿ ಐಕ್ಯವಾಗು"
    - Also: "ಐಭೋಗದಲ್ಲಿ ನಿನಗೆ ಜಾತ್ರೆ ಆಗಲಿ, ಎರಡನೆಯ ಪೂಜೆ ಶಿವರಾತ್ರಿಯೊಳಗೆ ನಿಮಗಾಗಲಿ"
    - Prajavani (30 Dec 2025, fact 37) puts the 2026 version this way: "ಸಿದ್ದಪ್ಪಾಜಿಯು ಪಾಂಚಾಳರು ಒಡ್ಡುವ ಸವಾಲು, ಪವಾಡಗಳನ್ನು ಗೆದ್ದು ಏಳು ಊರುಗಳ ಪಾಳೆಪಟ್ಟುಗಳನ್ನು ಒಕ್ಕಲು ಪಡೆದು ಚಿಕ್ಕಲೂರಿನಲ್ಲಿ ನೆಲೆಸುತ್ತಾರೆ."

26. **A rival "historical" retelling (DH 2019, from local lore and K P Swamy's novel):**

    - Kempachari does 12 years' tapas in a Kunduru cave and enters Kaloj's magnetic fort as a bangle-seller.
    - Kaloj surrenders, becomes his disciple and writes *Nilagara Vidhi Chandrike* under the name Ghanneela.

28. **Siddappaji and Muttattiraya:**
    - Before the Halaguru miracle, Siddappaji won the friendship of Muttattiraya, whom he calls ಭಾವ.
    - Muttattiraya gives him a tamburi to beg with "in your own caste's street". The tradition says the tamburi itself came from Muttattiraya.
    - In return, Siddappaji promises a ಮುತ್ತತ್ತಿರಾಯ ಸೇವೆ at Chikkalluru. This is the jatre's last day (fact 37).
    - Tier: legend
    - Source: Hiriyanna, epic text p. ೧೫೨; variant-notes §24 and jatre chapter p. ೨೪೨ (URL as fact 2)
    - Quote: "ಭಾವ ನಿಮಗೆ ಮುತ್ತತ್ತಿರಾಯ ಸೇವೆ ಅಂತ ಒಂದು ಸೇವೆ ಮಾಡಿಸ್ತೀನಿ ಚಿಕ್ಕಲ್ಲೂರಲ್ಲಿ."
    - Second quote: "ತಂಬೂರಿ ಮುತ್ತತ್ತಿರಾಯನಿಂದ ಬಂದಿತು"
    - Also: "ಹಲಗೂರು ಪವಾಡಕ್ಕೂ ಪೂರ್ವದಲ್ಲಿ ಮುತ್ತತ್ತಿರಾಯನ ಸಖ್ಯವನ್ನು ಸಿದ್ದಪ್ಪಾಜಿಯವರು ಗಳಿಸಿರುವುದು ಕಾವ್ಯದಲ್ಲಿ ನಿರೂಪಿತವಾಗಿದೆ."

29. **The epic prescribes the jatre. Speaking to Muttattiraya, Siddappaji says:**
    - On "the very first day of the full moon" they will light a world-flame called Chandramandala, and it is for Channajamma.
    - Day 2 is ದೊಡ್ಡೋರ ಸೇವೆ, a rice-and-payasa meal for Manteswamy, Rachappaji and Doddammatayi.
    - Day 3 is the devotees' ಮುಡಿ ಸೇವೆ, for Madivala Machappa.
    - Day 4 is "ಭಂಗಿಸೇವೆ" for himself.
    - Tier: legend
    - Source: Hiriyanna, epic text pp. ೧೫೧–೧೫೨ (URL as fact 2)
    - Quote: "ಹುಣ್ಣಿಮೆಯ ಮೊದ್‌ ಮೊದಲನೆಯ ದಿನ ಚಂದ್ರಮಂಡಲ ಅಂತ ಜಗನ್‌ಜ್ಯೋತಿ ಹತ್ತಿಸುತ್ತಾರೆ"
    - Second quote: "ಯಾತ್ರಿಕರೆಲ್ಲ ಭಕ್ತಾದಿಗಳೆಲ್ಲ ಮುಡಿ ಸೇವೆ ಕೊಡುತ್ತಾರೆ."

## C. Manteswamy: who he is, disciples, places, date

30. **The epic, also called "Dharege doddavara kathe", centres on Manteswamy, the "Dharege doddavaru". He is a folk hero of about the 15th century, with little documentary evidence. The shrines at Chikkelluru, Boppagoudanapura and Kappadi are tied to the epic's characters.**
    - Tier: scholarship (reference database)
    - Source: shastriyakannada.org (URL as fact 25)
    - Quote: "Manteswamy was a folk hero who lived during the fifteenth century.(approximately) Not much information is found about his life by way of documentary evidence."
    - Also: "Kalyana.Chikkelluru, (cikkellUru) Boppagoudanapura (boppagaVDana pura) and Kappadi (kappaDi) are the main centres associated with Manteswamy."

31. **Opening scene: Manteswamy appears on a dung heap at the gate of Kalyana with a dead calf and a gourd of toddy. Basavanna's wife takes him in.**
    - Tier: legend
    - Source: shastriyakannada.org (URL as fact 25)
    - Quote: "who makes a dramatic appearance on a dung heap at the entrance of the city, with the dead body of a calf and a gourd full of kaLLu."
    - Hiriyanna (p. ೧೯೫) frames the opening differently: Neelalochane asks Basavanna "who is your guru?" and Basavanna admits he has none.

32. **Chief disciples: Rachappaji, Doddamma "of the Grove", Channajamma, Madivala Machayya, Phalaradayya and Siddappaji. Manteswamy "picks up infants" (ಶಿಶುಮಕ್ಕಳು), and each child stands for a community joining the fold.**
    - Tier: scholarship (reference, citing S. R. Ramakrishna)
    - Source: shastriyakannada.org (URL as fact 25)
    - Quote: "Rachappaji, Doddamma of the Grove, Channajamma, Madivala Machayya, Phalaradayya and Siddappaji are his chief followers."
    - Second quote: "Each child represents a community that comes into the Manteswamy fold."

33. **The gaddiges along the Kaveri: Manteswamy at Boppegowdanapura, Rachappaji and Channajamma at Kappadi, Nanjayya and Channayya at Kurubana Katte, Siddappaji at Chikkalluru.**
    - Tier: scholarship (peer-reviewed article)
    - Source: M. S. Chandra (TISS) & Remya V. P. (Central University of Karnataka), "Siddhapatha of Manteswamy: The Great Journey of Light", *The Eastern Anthropologist* 75:1-2 (2022), pp. 55–61
    - URL: https://serialsjournals.com/abstract/79279_6-siddapatha_of_manteswamy.pdf
    - Quote: "Manteswamy’s gaddige is at Boppegowdanapura, gaddige of Rachappaji and Channajamma are at Kappadi"
    - Also: "Gaddige or tomb of Manteswamy is in Boppe Gowdana Pura of Kollegal Taluk of Chamrajnagar District"
    - Location note: this article places Boppegowdanapura in Kollegal taluk. Hiriyanna (p. ೧೯೫ field-work section) calls it "ಮಂಡ್ಯಜಿಲ್ಲೆಯ ಬೊಪ್ಪೇಗೌಡನಪುರ". Templeinkarnataka says Malavalli taluk. Use "near Malavalli" safely (DH 2017, fact 34).

34. **Route: the journey from Kodekal (Yadgir district) to Boppegowdanapura, "twenty-one years" and "around seven hundred kilometers". The path runs along rivers from the Krishna basin to the Kaveri basin.**
    - Tier: scholarship (article; a scholar's reconstruction of a legendary journey)
    - Source: *The Eastern Anthropologist* 2022 (URL as fact 33)
    - Quote: "This journey took Twenty-one years of grand journey to cover around seven hundred kilometers from Kodekal of Yadgir district to Boppegowdanapura"
    - The annual pilgrimage today: DH 2017 (fact 45) says it starts at Chikkaloor after Sankranti, then "the pilgrimage moves to Bappegowdanapura near Malavalli and later on to Kappadi near K R Nagara", ending at Ugadi.

35. **The Manteswamy line is not a single person's story. Rachappaji (son of Kodekal Basavanna), Doddamma Tayi from Kaginele, Phalaharadayya, Ningayya, Channayya and Channajamma join on the way.**
    - Tier: legend (as summarised by scholars)
    - Source: *The Eastern Anthropologist* 2022 (URL as fact 33)
    - Quote: "Rachappaji son of Kodekal Basavanna, Doddamma Tayi from Kaginele, Phalaharadayya, Ningayya, Channayya and Channajamma are some of important shishu makkalu"
    - Hiriyanna's variant-notes (§48) record a rival story for Doddammatayi: most southern texts have her given as a ಶಿಶುಮಗಳು at Mysuru, to save her from a forced marriage to the Karugahalli Maranayaka's son.

36. **Date and the 12th-century link (scholarship):**
    - The epic equates Manteswamy with Allama Prabhu. Shastriya Kannada says "this contention does not find many takers".
    - Hiriyanna: no evidence he belonged to the Basava movement or was contemporary with Basavanna or Allama, but "enough evidence" that he lived in its later phase.
    - Shankanapura (Andolana 2024): 15th–16th century, linked with the Vijayanagara and Mysore Wodeyar periods.
    - Tier: scholarship
    - Source: shastriyakannada.org (URL as fact 25)
    - Quote: "Manteswamy Kavya treats him on par with Allamaprabhu the Veerashiava saint of the twelfth century and does not distinguish between them. However this contention does not find many takers."
    - Hiriyanna, introduction p. xvii (URL as fact 2): "ಈತ ಬಸವಣ್ಣ ಅಲ್ಲಮಾದಿಗಳ ಸಮಕಾಲೀನನಾಗಿದ್ದ ಎಂಬುದಕ್ಕಾಗಲೀ ಯಾವುದೇ ಆಧಾರಗಳು ಸಿಕ್ಕುವುದಿಲ್ಲ."
    - Andolana 2024 (fact 27 URL): "15-16ನೇ ಶತಮಾನದ ಈ ಪರಂಪರೆಯ ಸಂತರ ಚರಿತ್ರೆ ಬೆಸೆದುಕೊಂಡಿರುವುದು ಕುತೂಹಲಕರವಾಗಿದೆ."
    - Andolana 2024 also says palm-leaf texts survive: 13 ಕಾಲಜ್ಞಾನ ವಚನ under the signature ಮಂಟೇದಲಿಂಗ, plus 10 kavyas and 46 tatvapadas by Kappadi Rachappaji.

## D. The Neelagaras

37. **Who and where: Neelagaras live in Mysuru, Chamarajanagar, Mandya, Ramanagara and Bengaluru (Rural) districts. They are called ಮಂಟೇದಯ್ಯ or ಮಂಟೇದವರು, have maths at Malavalli, Boppegowdanapura and Chikkalluru, and call themselves "Siddappaji's ಗುಡ್ಡರು".**
    - Tier: scholarship
    - Source: Hiriyanna, introduction pp. xvii–xviii (URL as fact 2)
    - Quote: "ಇವರು ತಮ್ಮನ್ನು ಸಿದ್ಧಪ್ಪಾಜಿಯ ಗುಡ್ಡಗಳು ಎಂದು ಕರೆದುಕೊಳ್ಳುತ್ತಾರೆ."
    - Also: "ಮಳವಳ್ಳಿ, ಬೊಪ್ಪೇಗೌಡನಪುರ ಹಾಗೂ ಚಿಕ್ಕಲ್ಲೂರಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಇವರ ಮಠಗಳಿವೆ."
    - Sensitive: the same paragraph gives a caste composition. See "Sensitive points".

38. **The diksha at the jatre:**
    - Families who keep Manteswamy, Rachappaji, Doddammatayi, Channajamma or Siddappaji as house-god have their eldest son initiated.
    - The initiation comes from the math's head. It consists of wearing a rudraksha bead, with instruction in a pure, renouncing life.
    - It symbolises the Halaguru Panchalas' final turn to renunciation.
    - Tier: scholarship
    - Source: Hiriyanna, jatre chapter p. ೨೪೧ (URL as fact 2)
    - Quote: "ಭಕ್ತಾದಿಗಳು ತಮ್ಮ ಕುಟುಂಬದ ಹಿರಿಯ ಮಗನಿಗೆ ಈ ದೀಕ್ಷೆಯನ್ನು ಕೊಡಿಸುತ್ತಾರೆ."
    - Also: "ಇದು ಹಲಗೂರು ಪಾಂಚಾಳರ ಅಂತಿಮ"
    - Diksha also happens at Kappadi, Boppegowdanapura and the Channapatna Hale Matha (p. ೧೯೫).

39. **Children are initiated. On day 3 of the 2026 jatre "some had the Neelagara diksha given to their children", and thousands of men and women had their heads tonsured.**
    - Tier: scholarship (news)
    - Source: Prajavani, "ಚಿಕ್ಕಲ್ಲೂರು: ಜಾತ್ರೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಭಕ್ತರ ದಂಡು, ವ್ಯಾಪಾರ ಜೋರು", 6 Jan 2026
    - URL: https://www.prajavani.net/district/mysuru/chikkalluru-siddappaji-jatra-devotees-crowd-kollegala-fair-3714686
    - Quote: "ಕೆಲವರು ಮಕ್ಕಳಿಗೆ ನೀಲಗಾರ ದೀಕ್ಷೆ ಕೊಡಿಸಿದರು."
    - Second quote: "ಗಂಡು-ಹೆಣ್ಣು ಎಂಬ ಭೇದವಿಲ್ಲದೆ ಸಾವಿರಾರು ಭಕ್ತರು ಕೇಶಮುಂಡನ ಮಾಡಿಸಿಕೊಂಡು ಹರಕೆ ತೀರಿಸಿದರು."
    - *The Eastern Anthropologist* 2022 (fact 33): "Training begin with initiation into group, generally eldest son of the family." Also: "Initiation is given to people from any caste or class without any discrimination whoever has interest in it."

40. **Dress:**
    - A big red turban.
    - A white shirt with a black coat over it, and a white panche.
    - A ಕಾವಿ cloth on the shoulder.
    - A single blue-faced rudraksha at the neck.
    - Bronze rings on two fingers of the left hand.
    - A four-string tamburi with a cobra-hood head on the shoulder.
    - Ash stripes with a black dot on the forehead.
    - Barefoot.
    - Tier: scholarship
    - Source: Hiriyanna, p. ೨೧೯–೨೨೦ (URL as fact 2)
    - Quote: "ಮೇಲೆ ನಾಗರ ಹೆಡೆಯ ನಾಲ್ಕು ತಂತಿಯ ತಂಬೂರಿ"
    - Second quote: "ಬರಿಗಾಲಿನಲ್ಲಿಯೇ ಲೋಕ ಸಂಚಾರಕ್ಕೆ ಹೋಗುತ್ತಾರೆ."
    - The field-work summary (p. ೧೯೫) gives the short form: "ತಲೆಗೆ ಕೆಂಪುಪೇಟ, ಕರಿನಿಲುವಂಗಿ, ಬಿಳಿಯ ಪಿಲ್ಲಿ ಹಾಗೂ ಬಗಳಿಗೊಂದು ಜೋಳಿಗೆ."

41. **Instruments: a four-string tamburi plus ದಮ್ಮಡಿ, ಗಗ್ಗರ and ಗುಮ್ಮಟೆ. Another singer's list is tamburi, dakki, tala, gaggara, kanchi and ghatam. Some now add keyboard and "ಕಮ್ಜ್‌" (OCR, probably a conga-type drum).**
    - Tier: scholarship
    - Source: Hiriyanna, p. ೧೯೫ and singer profiles pp. ೨೬೯–೨೭೨ (URL as fact 2)
    - Quote: "ನಾಲ್ಕು ತಂತಿಯ ತಂಬೂರಿ, ದಮ್ಮಡಿ, ಗಗ್ಗರ ಹಾಗೂ ಗುಮ್ಮಟೆ ಇವೇ ಮುಖ್ಯವಾದುದು."
    - Second quote: "ತಂಬೂರಿ, ಡಕ್ಕಿ , ತಾಳ, ಗಗ್ರ , ಕಂಚಿ, ಘಟಂ"
    - Kamsale: no source says Neelagaras play kamsale. TV9 2021 lists ಕಂಸಾಳೆ in the ಹಳೇ ಮಠ procession band (fact 52).

42. **How it is sung:**

    - The style mixes poetry, drama and prose.
    - Tier: scholarship

## F. Pankti seve

59. **The story behind it, from the epic tradition:**
    - ದೊಡ್ಡವರ ಪಂಕ್ತಿಸೇವೆ, also called ದೊಡ್ಡಮ್ಮತಾಯಿ ಪಂಕ್ತಿಸೇವೆ, teaches Manteswamy's anna-dasoha.
    - Doddammatayi seated all comers in rows and served ragi mudde with ಅಣ್ಣೆಸೊಪ್ಪು saaru.
    - The Halaguru Panchalas came, sneered, and trampled the food with their sandalled feet.
    - Doddamma wept, and Manteswamy cursed them.
    - Tier: legend
    - Source: Hiriyanna, pp. ೨೪೧–೨೪೨ (URL as fact 2)
    - Quote: "ದೊಡ್ಡಮ್ಮತಾಯಿ ಆರ್ಕದ ಮುದ್ದೆ ಅಣ್ಣೆಸೊಪ್ಪು ಸಾರು ಮಾಡಿ ಬಂದ ಜನರಿಗೆಲ್ಲ ಸಾಲಾಗಿ ಕೂರಿಸಿ ಅಡಿಗೆ ಬಡಿಸುತ್ತಿದ್ದರು."
    - The Panchalas' line: "ನಾವು ಹಾಲು ಅನ್ನ ತಿಂದು ತುಪ್ಪದಲ್ಲಿ ಕೈತೊಳೆಯುವವರು."
    - Note: this scholarly explanation is vegetarian, a millet meal. The meat custom at Chikkalluru is reported only by the news (facts 60–62).

## H. Social meaning (attributed interpretation)

73. **Shastriya Kannada: the epic is part of the oral tradition of Dalits, and many non-Dalits also recite it. It is told in a dialect "both geographically and socially" chosen. It portrays "unlettered communities" absent from earlier Kannada literature.**
    - Tier: scholarship
    - Source: shastriyakannada.org (URL as fact 25)
    - Quote: "A part of the oral tradition of Dalits. (Many non dalits also recite it.)"
    - Second quote: "This work delineates the life of unlettered communities which were conspicuous by their absence in the entire history of Kannada literature."

74. **The invocation sung by Neelagaras lists the houses of many communities and ends with the Light glowing "without any discrimination" on the houses of "seven kula and eighteen castes". Compare "ಕುಲೇಳು 18 ಸಮುದಾಯಗಳು", who jointly hold the Chandramandala (fact 51).**
    - Tier: scholarship (translation of the epic's invocation, citing Rajashekara 2018)
    - Source: *The Eastern Anthropologist* 2022 (URL as fact 33)
    - Quote: "Your Great light will glow on the all houses of seven kula (clan) and eighteen castes without any discrimination and division."
    - Same article: "Light in the houses of poor / Light in the houses of wealthy".
    - The authors read it as a struggle for "sarvarigu samapalu and sarvarigu samabalu" (an equal share for all, and equal strength for all).

75. **Shankanapura's editorial (Andolana 2024): the jatre enacts caste-annihilation. People of almost all castes are Neelagara ಒಕ್ಕಲು and worship at one gaddige. He quotes the epic line "that caste and this caste become one caste".**
    - Tier: scholarship (signed editorial; interpretation)
    - Source: Andolana, 25 Jan 2024 (URL as fact 27)
    - Quote: "ಆ ಜಾತಿ ಈ ಜಾತಿ ಏಕ ಜಾತಿ ಆಗುತ್ತದೆ. ಮೇಲು ಕೀಳಾಗುತ್ತದೆ, ಕೀಳು ಮೇಲಾಗುತ್ತದೆ’"
    - Also: "ಎಲ್ಲೂ ಭೇದ ಭಾವ ಜಾತೀಯತೆಗೆ ಆಸ್ಪದವಿಲ್ಲ."

76. **Hiriyanna's reading: Manteswamy "was not a saint just filling his belly". His aim seems to have been a counter-culture restoring the lower classes against entrenched culture. The tradition's heroes "fought for equality" and are now worshipped. Neelagaras are "folk teachers" of equality.**
    - Tier: scholarship
    - Source: Hiriyanna, introduction p. xvii and p. ೨೧೯ (URL as fact 2)
    - Quote: "ಮಂಟೇಸ್ವಾಮಿ ಕೇವಲ ಹೊಟ್ಟೆಹೊರೆಯುವ ಸಂತನಾಗಿರಲಿಲ್ಲ."
    - Second quote: "ಐತಿಹಾಸಿಕ ವ್ಯಕ್ತಿಗಳಾಗಿ ಸಮಾಜದ ಸಮಾನತೆಗಾಗಿ ಹೋರಾಡಿ ಸಮಸಂಸ್ಕೃತಿಯನ್ನು ಕಟ್ಟಲು ಹೆಣಗಾಡಿದವರು."

77. **The plough reading (DH 2017): a blacksmith family's monopoly on the iron plough is broken when Manteswamy wins Siddappaji over. The folk-culture expert Sundar Kenaje calls it "a major social revolution". Mahadeva Shankanapura calls Manteswamy "a social reformer of the highest degree" who challenged priest-mediated Vedic worship.**
    - Tier: scholarship (feature, quoting experts)
    - Source: Deccan Herald, 14 Aug 2017 (URL as fact 45)
    - Quote: "Lord Manteswamy facilitated the dissemination of this technology by winning over Siddapaji, a son of the blacksmith family, as his disciple!"
    - Second quote: "“Manteswamy is a social reformer of the highest degree,” opines Mahadeva Shankanapura"

## Disputes and gaps

**Disputes**

1. **Siddappaji's parents and earlier birth.**
   - (a) Bachibasavachari and Muddamma of Nidughatta/Maralli. He is the rebirth of the Kalyana gate-guard Katukara Sangayya. Hiriyanna's epic text and notes §28, §32, §65; Shankanapura 2024 also names "ಬಾಚಿ ಬಸವಣ್ಣಚಾರಿ- ಮುದ್ದಮ್ಮ".
   - (b) He is the rebirth of **Baachi Basavayya** himself (Shastriya Kannada; templeinkarnataka blog, which names the parents Muddoji and Lingamma).
   - (c) He is the son of "Muddoji", the father-in-law of King Kaloj (DH 2019).
   - The epic also calls the mother "ಮುದ್ದೋಜಿ" once, p. ೭೭. Use (a) for the story and note the others.
2. **Where Manteswamy got his name.** At Kempanna's mother Muddamma's house in Maralli (Hiriyanna's main text), or at Bale Muddamma's house in Adihonnayakanahalli (texts ಟ, ತ, ಪ; templeinkarnataka). Whether Bale Muddamma is the same woman is unclear.
3. **Which full moon.** Hiriyanna: ಪುಷ್ಯ ಹುಣ್ಣಿಮೆ (OCR "ಪುಷ್ಕಪೂರ್ಣಿಮೆ"). DH 2013: full moon of "Maga masa". Blog: the January full moon around Sankranti. DH 2013: "every year between January 27 and 31", which is contradicted by the 2021, 2024, 2025 and 2026 dates. The 2025 and 2026 start dates (13 Jan 2025, 3 Jan 2026) fit a Pushya full moon.
4. **Who builds the Chandramandala.**
   - Prajavani 2025: the Gurumane Neelagaras, from materials given by the villages.
   - Hiriyanna: the seven villages build it.
   - TV9 2025: only the ಆದಿಜಾಂಬವ community does it.
   - The number of villages: Prajavani says seven but lists six; Oneindia 2017 says ten villages hold temple rights.
5. **Day names.** Day 2: ಹುಲಿವಾಹನ + ದೊಡ್ಡವರ ಸೇವೆ (Prajavani), or ದೊಡ್ಡಮ್ಮತಾಯಿ ಸೇವೆ (Andolana 2024), or ದೊಡ್ಡಮ್ಮ ತಾಯಿ and ರಾಚಪ್ಪಾಜಿ sevas on days 1–2 (DH 2021). Day 3: ರುದ್ರಾಕ್ಷಿ ಮಂಟಪೋತ್ಸವ / ಮಂಟಪೋತ್ಸವ (ಮುಡಿಸೇವೆ / ನೀಲಗಾರ ದೀಕ್ಷೆ). DH 2021 puts the Chandramandala jyothi on the night of day 3, while all other sources put it on night 1. Day 5: ಮುತ್ತುರಾಯರ / ಮುತ್ತರಾಯನ / ಮುತ್ತತ್ತಿರಾಯನ ಸೇವೆ, or ಕಡೆ ಬಾಗಿಲು ಸೇವೆ. The epic's own day-4 offering is "ಭಂಗಿಸೇವೆ" (fact 29).
6. **Pankti seve food.** Hiriyanna explains it as ragi mudde and ಅಣ್ಣೆಸೊಪ್ಪು saaru (Doddammatayi's dasoha). The news consistently reports a family meat meal offered as ಎಡೆ. Shankanapura (2018) says sacrifice is not part of the jatre, while the petitioner says on-site cooking equals sacrifice. A 2017 interim HC order permitted pankti seve, but the administration still enforced the ban in 2018–2024. How the interim order ended is unknown.
7. **Manteswamy's date and the 12th-century link.**
   - About the 15th century (Shastriya; *The Eastern Anthropologist*).
   - 15th–16th century (Shankanapura; Minister Mahadevappa 2026).
   - The epic equates him with Allama Prabhu and has him meet Basavanna. Scholars reject literal contemporaneity (Shastriya, Hiriyanna).
   - One variant-note puts the Mysore king "ಚಾಮರಾಜ ಒಡೆಯರ (ಕ್ರಿಶ.೧೩೬೦)" inside the story. This is an editor's gloss and clashes with the 15th-century dating.
8. **Length of a full recital.** "ಮೂರರಿಂದ ಐದು ರಾತ್ರಿ" (Hiriyanna), a 24-hour full recording (Hiriyanna), or "10 days and 10 nights" (*The Eastern Anthropologist*).
9. **Location of Boppegowdanapura.** Kollegal taluk (*The Eastern Anthropologist*), Mandya district (Hiriyanna), Malavalli taluk (templeinkarnataka), or "near Malavalli" (DH 2017).
10. **Historicity of Siddappaji.** Legend only (the epic), "according to historians" (DH 2019, unsourced), or a possible historical Siddappayya cave at Galaga, Raichur (Shankanapura, hedged).
11. **Age of the jatre.** "600 years" (TV9 2025, unsourced) or "ದಶಕಗಳಿಂದ" (Prajavani). Use neither as fact.

## Sensitive points (for the writer)

- **Caste labels that sources state:**
  - Shastriya Kannada: "oral tradition of Dalits", with non-Dalits also reciting.
  - Hiriyanna, p. xvii: Neelagaras are mainly "ಹರಿಜನ", and also Kurubas, Uppaliga Shettis, Madivalas, Akkasaligas, Parivara and Kumbaras. Note that "ಹರಿಜನ" is a dated term.
  - Hiriyanna, Muttatti: Madigas have the right to the ಹಾಲರವಿ ಸೇವೆ, and Soligas are the priests at Muttattiraya's temple.
  - Hiriyanna, epic note §63: "ಹೊಲೆಮಾದಿಗರವನಾದ ಲಿಂಗಯ್ಯ ಚೆನ್ನಯ್ಯ".
  - TV9 2025: only the ಆದಿಜಾಂಬವ community builds the Chandramandala.
  - Star of Mysore 2022: Malavalli Mahadevaswamy described as "a Dalit singer".
  - The Panchalas / Vishwakarma blacksmiths are the antagonists of the Halaguru episode, and Siddappaji is himself from that community. Frame it as a story of a smith's son and his own people, not a caste villain.
  - The minister and Shankanapura stress that the jatre has no caste divisions.
- **Animal sacrifice:** this is a live legal and religious dispute. Keep it factual:
  - The law (s. 3).
  - The HC order of 20 Jan 2016 and the interim order of 13 Jan 2017.
  - The checkposts and seizures.
  - Devotees' "no bali peetha / right to food" argument.
  - The animal-welfare side's argument.
  - Avoid gore in a children's book. Pankti seve can be told through Doddamma's mudde-and-saaru story (fact 59) plus "families cook and eat together".
- **Bhangi (cannabis):** Manteswamy smokes bhangi in key scenes (facts 14–15). The epic names a "ಭಂಗಿಸೇವೆ". Oneindia 2023 says bhangi was formerly placed in the offering.
- **Violence and gore in the epic:** Madivala Machayya killing his wife Mallige Mahadevi to dye the kavi cloth in her blood, and serving his son (Hiriyanna §39, §49). The curse that brings 7771 Maris on Maralli. The rules on women at the jatre (Doddamma's menstruation episode, §-notes). Leave these out or soften them, in line with the series' keep-source-story rule.
- **The 2016 named accused (fact 67):** keep them out of the book.
