# Research brief (shared by every series file)

Goal: a sourced fact sheet that a writer can turn into a substantial Kannada history story (book
+ animated screenplay) for adult readers WITHOUT adding anything unsourced. Facts only.
Every claim must carry a source URL (or book + page) and a tier:
- `inscription`: inscription, coin, contemporary chronicle, eyewitness (quote the line if short)
- `scholarship`: historians' accepted conclusion from primary evidence (name the historian/work)
- `legend`: traditional story with no contemporary evidence (say where the legend is first recorded)
Mark disputed points as disputed, with both sides. No dates, numbers or names from memory.

Write the file in English with Kannada names in Kannada script beside each (ಇಮ್ಮಡಿ ಪುಲಿಕೇಶಿ).
Sections, in this order:
1. Timeline and rulers (dates with source)
2. Primary sources: what survives (inscriptions, copper plates, travellers, chronicles, coins),
   where to read them online, and licence status (public-domain editions such as Epigraphia
   Carnatica/Indica, Fleet 1896, Beal 1884, Sewell 1900, Kirkpatrick 1811 are usable; modern
   books only as references, never to copy)
3. Candidate stories: 3 to 5, each with: the people, the arc in 5 to 8 beats, the one goosebump
   moment and the evidence it rests on, what is dramatised vs recorded, and why it would grip adults
4. Little-known facts: 8 to 12, each one line + source + tier
5. Disputed or uncertain points
6. Visual references for accurate AI images: dress, armour, weapons, ships, elephants/horses,
   architecture of the period, dynastic emblem, surviving sculpture/relief/coin/portrait with
   links (Wikimedia Commons file pages preferred), plus things that would be anachronistic
7. Key people beyond the kings (poets, queens, generals, saints, travellers) with one line each
8. Sources to avoid (popular pages with known errors, uncited blogs)

Target 600 to 1200 lines of dense, cited notes. Quality over length. Prefer primary editions
and academic sources; Wikipedia only as a finding aid, follow its citations to the real source.
