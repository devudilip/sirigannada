# Visual bible: Gangavadi and Shravanabelagola, 10th century CE (ಚಾವುಂಡರಾಯ)

Every prompt pastes the STYLE LINE, the relevant CHARACTER and SETTING blocks verbatim, and the
EXCLUSIONS. Sources: research/ganga.md §6 (the Begur, Atakur and Doddahundi hero stones for dress
and arms; Rice's description of the colossus; Buchanan on the 18th-century tank).

## STYLE LINE (paste verbatim)
Cinematic photorealistic historical still, 16:9, 10th-century southern Karnataka. Dramatic natural
light, haze and dust where the scene calls for it, shallow depth of field on people, high detail in
granite, cloth and skin. Colour: grey-white granite, red laterite earth, dry scrub green, white
cotton, dull gold, unpolished iron. No text, captions, logos or watermarks. Not a painting, not
cartoon, not watercolor, not a film-poster look.

## CHARACTERS (locked; reference renders in the video folder bible/characters/)

### chavundaraya-general (scenes 1–3)
Age 40–45. No portrait survives (the Tyagada pillar relief is too worn), so the face is invented:
handsome and strong. Medium-dark brown skin, tall, broad shoulders and a hard muscular build,
square jaw, straight strong nose, thick black moustache and NO beard, black hair pulled back into
a knot with a little grey at the temples, an old pale scar on the left forearm, a cloth fillet of office with an upright plume (as on the Begur stone), bare chest with a
cloth sash, gold earrings and armlets, white dhoti with a red border, straight sword, a dagger at
the side. Hard, watchful face.

### chavundaraya-minister (scenes 4, 6–8)
Same face, 45–55, grey at the temples, the plume fillet, a white uttariya over the shoulder,
fewer ornaments, a palm-leaf bundle or a stylus in hand. Calm.

### marasimha
Age 50. Ganga king: tall crown, pearls, bare chest, patterned dhoti; in scene 2 seated in fast,
thin, ornaments laid aside, white cloth only.

### rachamalla
Age 20. The young king: crown, pearls, nervous.

### ajitasena / nemichandra
Digambara Jain monks: nude, shaven head, a peacock-feather whisk (pichchi) and a water gourd
(kamandalu), calm, seated on a stone bench. Shown with tact (seated, side view, in shadow or
behind a pillar); no close frontal nudity.

### bard (scenes 5, 9)
A 16th-century Jain storyteller at the foot of the hill: old man in a white dhoti and turban-wrap,
a small drum, pilgrims around him; marks the legend scenes.

### buchanan (scene 12, optional)
Francis Buchanan, 1800: a Scottish doctor in a broad hat, white shirt and breeches, eyes bandaged
or shaded, sitting on a rock at the hill's foot while a painter sketches.

## SETTINGS (locked)

### belgola-hills
Two bare granite hills across a small valley with a village between: Vindhyagiri (higher, c. 470
ft above the plain) and Chandragiri (lower, with a cluster of small Jain shrines). In 10th-century
scenes: NO enclosure wall or cloister round the colossus (Gangaraja's, 1116), NO large tank in the
village (18th century), NO Hoysala-style temples, NO modern town.

### colossus
57 ft nude standing figure (kayotsarga) cut from the living grey granite of Vindhyagiri's crown,
facing north; anthills with emerging serpents up to the thighs; a madhavi creeper twining round the
legs and arms ending in clusters of fruit; open-lotus pedestal; hair in spiral ringlets flat to the
head; long ear-lobes; eyes half-open; faint smile. Shown with tact: from the side, from below, or
with scaffolding and figures in front at waist height.

### chandragiri-shrines
Small plain granite basadis with flat or low pyramidal roofs, pillared porches, a tall monolithic
pillar (manastambha or brahmadeva pillar) with a small figure on top.

### war-camp, uchchangi
Dry Deccan hill country, a rock hill-fort with rough stone walls on a crag, torches, elephants
with plain cloth, small horses with cloth saddles.

### talakadu-court
A timber pillared hall by the Kaveri; low wooden throne; the Ganga elephant on a cloth banner.

## PERIOD RULES
- Dress from the hero stones: fillet with upright plume for chiefs, royal umbrellas (two for a king),
  female chowrie-bearers, dagger at the side, straight swords, bows, round shields, no mail, no helmets.
- Elephants as royal mounts; small horses; no stirrups shown.
- Jain monks nude (Digambara), shown with tact; nuns in white.
- Scripts on stone: 10th-century Old Kannada (rounded, bold), Grantha/Vatteluttu Tamil, Nagari;
  abstract ancient strokes only, never modern glyphs.
- Banner: the Ganga elephant on plain cloth; no lion, boar or gandabherunda.

## EXCLUSIONS (paste verbatim)
No enclosure wall round the colossus before 1116, no Hoysala or Vijayanagara architecture, no large
tank at Belgola, no modern buildings or crowds in 10th-century scenes, no helmets, mail or curved
swords, no stirrups, no turbans of Mughal type, no modern script on stone, no close frontal nudity of
the colossus or the monks, no gore in close-up, no named sculptor shown as a hero.
