# ಚಾವುಂಡರಾಯ: working outline (book and screenplay share this)

Source spine: the Shravanabelagola inscriptions in B. L. Rice, Epigraphia Carnatica II (1889,
public domain; revised by Narasimhachar 1923): nos. 75–76 (the signature lines under the colossus),
109 (Chavundaraya's own eulogy on the Tyagada Brahmadeva pillar), 38 (Marasimha's epitaph on the
Kuge Brahmadeva pillar), 67 (the basadi), 89 (the three ministers); Hultzsch EI VII (1902) on the
three-script lines; the Chavundaraya Purana colophon (Saka 900 = 978); Buchanan 1807; Rice's 1889
introduction. Research: research/ganga.md and research/ganga-verified.md.

Dating: the 10th-century records are dated. 974/975 (Marasimha's death), 978 (Purana), 981 or 983
(the colossus; disputed, both given), c. 995 (basadi), 1116 (Gangaraja's enclosure), 1800
(Buchanan), 1871 (Scanlon's measurement), 1889 (Rice).

Title (working): ಕಪಾಲದ ಬಟ್ಟಲು, ಗೊಮ್ಮಟನ ನಗು: ಚಾವುಂಡರಾಯ (The Skull-Cup and Gommata's Smile: Chavundaraya)
Subtitle: ಗಂಗ ಸೇನಾನಿ, ಗೊಮ್ಮಟೇಶ್ವರನನ್ನು ಕೆತ್ತಿಸಿದವನು, ೧೦ನೇ ಶತಮಾನ

Legend rule for this book: two scenes are legend (the arrow, the old woman's milk). Each is told
as ಐತಿಹ್ಯ inside the scene, and its image shows the legend being TOLD (a pilgrim or bard at the
foot of the hill in a later century), never the event as if recorded.

## ಪೀಠಿಕೆ
Gangavadi in the 10th century: Talakadu's Gangas as Rashtrakuta allies; Jainism at court; the two
hills at Belgola. The voice: the general's own pillar, his king's epitaph, three lines under a
statue's hands, and a Scottish doctor who climbed the hill in 1800 and could not see.

## ಸಾರಾಂಶ
Ten lines from war to the colossus to the measurements.

## ಇದು ಕಥೆ, ಇದು ಇತಿಹಾಸ
All dialogue; the camp, the throne hall, the writing desk, the scaffolds; the bard who tells the two
legends; Chavundaraya's face (the Tyagada relief is the only near-contemporary likeness, worn);
the manner of his death (unrecorded); which year the colossus was finished.

## ಇಂದು ನೋಡಬಹುದು
Shravanabelagola: Vindhyagiri (the colossus, the signature lines, Tyagada Brahmadeva pillar),
Chandragiri (Kuge Brahmadeva pillar, Chavundaraya basadi), the Belgola pond; Rice's EC II online.
