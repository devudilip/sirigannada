# ಚಾವುಂಡರಾಯ: outline (storytelling template, 2026-10-23)

Spine: Chavundaraya, the Ganga general who boasted in stone of skull-cups brimming with heroes' blood, watches his king Marasimha lay down the crown and fast to death, saves the young king's throne from Mudurachayya, his brother's killer, and then wants to leave something that is not blood; what stands in the way is a life with nothing in it but war, and his own pride; it turns at the colossus's feet when, instead of titles, lineage and wars, he has one line cut: his name and one word, ಮಾಡಿಸಿದಂ (had it made); the cost is his pride, and the sculptor's name lost for ever; today, every twelve years, milk runs down that smile to that single line.

Names in prose: ಚಾವುಂಡರಾಯ, ಮಾರಸಿಂಹ, ಮುದುರಾಚಯ್ಯ, and ಗೊಮ್ಮಟ (the image). Everyone else is a role.
