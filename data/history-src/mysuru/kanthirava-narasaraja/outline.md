# ಕಂಠೀರವ ನರಸರಾಜ ಒಡೆಯರ್: outline

Spine line: A wrestler's son who never expected the throne is crowned at 23, refuses the tribute every other fort has paid, and when Bijapur's greatest general breaches his island fort he climbs into the breach himself and saves Mysore.

Turning point: scene-09 (the breach held). Resolution: scene-12.
