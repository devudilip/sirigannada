# ಇಮ್ಮಡಿ ಪುಲಿಕೇಶಿ: book outline (2026-10-23 rewrite to README §6)

**Spine:** Pulikeshi, the boy who fled down Vatapi's rock stair by night from his jealous uncle Mangalesha, comes back by counsel and energy, takes the throne and wins three seas; Harsha, the emperor before whom every king of the north has bowed, comes south to break him; it turns on the bank of the Narmada, where he does not cross but waits, and Harsha's elephants fall and the north stops; the cost: the shamed Pallava's son churns Vatapi about 642 and no one records Pulikeshi's last day; but the Ganga princess's son brings the boar banner back, and the stone at Aihole still says of him: he rules.

Names in prose: ಪುಲಿಕೇಶಿ, ಮಂಗಳೇಶ, ಹರ್ಷ, ರವಿಕೀರ್ತಿ (same four as the screenplay). Years in prose: ಸುಮಾರು ೬೧೦, ೬೩೪, ಸುಮಾರು ೬೪೨.
