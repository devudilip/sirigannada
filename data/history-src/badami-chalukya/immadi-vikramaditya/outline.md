# ಉಳಿಸಿದ ಕಾಂಚಿ, ಮರಳಿಸಿದ ಚಿನ್ನ: ಇಮ್ಮಡಿ ವಿಕ್ರಮಾದಿತ್ಯ: outline (rewrite to the 2026-10-20 template, v2 with three new pictures)

Spine: Vikramaditya, a Chalukya prince raised on the story of the Pallavas churning Vatapi ninety years before, resolves on his crowning day to uproot that natural enemy and marches on Kanchi; in his way stand the Pallava king Nandipotavarman, who comes out to fight in the open, and, once he has won, his own revenge; it turns at the open gate of Kanchi, where he raises his hand, enters without destroying the city, gives the enemy temple's gold back to its god and has that cut in Kannada on its pillar; it costs him the ninety-year-old revenge and the victor's gold; his house remembers him as the conqueror of Kanchi, and his Kannada words still stand on the Kanchi pillar.

Names in the prose: ವಿಕ್ರಮಾದಿತ್ಯ, ನಂದಿಪೋತವರ್ಮ, ಲೋಕಮಹಾದೇವಿ (same three as the screenplay). Years in the prose: ಸುಮಾರು ೬೪೨, ೭೩೩ ಅಥವಾ ೭೩೪, ಸುಮಾರು ೭೪೫.
