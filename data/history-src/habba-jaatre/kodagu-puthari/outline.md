# ಪೊಲಿ ಪೊಲಿ ದೇವಾ: ಕೊಡಗಿನ ಪುತ್ತರಿ: outline

The book is in three parts:
- the legend of Igguthappa, as recorded from oral tradition in Richter's 1870 gazetteer;
- the festival night, told through one fictional Kodava family;
- a last page that separates legend from record.

The 2026 date is the night of 25 November, as fixed at Paadi on 9 November.

1. **ಘಟ್ಟ ದಾಟಿ ಬಂದವರು.** The siblings come from Malabar, and the brothers plot against their sister. At Chouripade, Igguthappa sets her a challenge: cook the rice with no fire.
2. **ಬೆಂಕಿಯಿಲ್ಲದ ಒಲೆ.** She asks that the rice be eaten without salt. She milks the Paradandra cow into the pot and buries it in the river sand.
3. **ಮರಳಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಕುದಿದ ಅನ್ನ.** The rice boils. He throws rice in the air as "hail"; she strikes him with the ladle as "thunder". Igguthappa is the god of rain and rice.
4. **ಮಾವಿನ ಮರಕ್ಕೆ ನಾಟಿದ ಬಾಣ.** The brothers trick her over betel, she weeps, and the arrow lands in the mango tree at Ponnangala. She flies there as a crane, and the whole family becomes gods. Igguthappa settles at Malma and Paadi.
5. **ಪಾಡಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಮುಹೂರ್ತ.** This is record. It covers:
   - the temple and its renovation in 1835;
   - how the date is fixed: astrologers from Ammangeri, the takkas, Birchiyar month and Rohini nakshatra, with Igguthappa's field first;
   - the 2026 decision;
   - the devara kattu and the old rule of peace.
6. **ಐನ್‌ಮನೆಗೆ ಮರಳುವ ದಾರಿ.** Dechamma, a fictional character, comes home. The page explains okka and ain mane, and the eed rehearsals.
7. **ನೆರೆ ಕಟ್ಟುವ ಹೊತ್ತು.** The rice-flour rangoli, the basket, the sickle, the taliyakki bolcha, the kudike pot and the five leaves of the nere. The timed sequence starts at Paadi.
8. **ದೀಪದ ಹಿಂದೆ ಗದ್ದೆಗೆ.** The sickle is handed over and the woman with the lamp leads, with the dudi players and the batte pat. The same order appears in the 1855 account.
9. **ಪೊಲಿ ಪೊಲಿ ದೇವಾ!** Milk and honey are poured, the nere is tied and the balo paat is sung. One shot is fired into the sky, an odd number of stalks are cut, and everyone gives the cry.
10. **ಹೊಸ ಅಕ್ಕಿ ಮನೆಗೆ.** The basket is carried on the head and the bearer's feet are washed. Ears are tied to everything in the house, then comes the payasa, thambittu and genasu feast.
11. **ಮಂದ್‌ನಲ್ಲಿ ಕೋಲಾಟ.** House-to-house songs, the kolata on the nad mand and at Madikeri Fort, and Urorme. Gowdas and other communities also celebrate.
12. **ಒಂದು ತೆನೆ, ಎರಡು ಕಥೆ.** Legend against record:
    - Moegling 1855, Richter 1870;
    - disputes over which leaves go into the nere, who cuts the first sheaf, and the temple's 1153 date;
    - the closing line.
