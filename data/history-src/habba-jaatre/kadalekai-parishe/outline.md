# ಹೊಲಕ್ಕೆ ಬಂದ ಬಸವ: ಕಡಲೆಕಾಯಿ ಪರಿಷೆ (outline)

The arc follows the legend in full, then the record. Doubts appear only on the last page.
1. Groundnut villages around today's Basavanagudi.
2. Ravaged fields and giant hoof prints.
3. Full-moon watch; the white bull arrives.
4. Chase up the hillock; the bull is stone.
5. The farmers vow their first crop.
6. The growing Nandi; the trident (or iron peg); the Vrishabhavathi spring.
7. Kempe Gowda's temple (1537 by tradition); the monolith; the name Basavanagudi.
8. The vow becomes a fair on the last Monday of Kartika.
9. Today's fair: 2025 figures, Malleshwaram, the plastic ban.
10. Legend and record; the groundnut came from South America, so the fair's groundnut form is probably younger.
