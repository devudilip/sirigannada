- **Look:** cinematic, photorealistic stills, 16:9.
- **Setting:** the Bengaluru plateau, with red earth, rounded granite boulders, village tanks, and mud-walled houses with clay-tiled roofs.
- **People:** Kannada farmers in white dhotis; women in cotton sarees.
- **Groundnut plants:** low and clover-leaved, with yellow flowers; the pods grow underground.
- **Legend scenes:** a moonlit white bull, and a stone Nandi rising out of the boulder.
- **The temple Nandi:** a seated monolith, glossy black, with bell chains. No trident is shown on it, because none is confirmed.
- **Never show:** gore, an injured animal, legible text, or a cartoon bull.
