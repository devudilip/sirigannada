# ಕೆಂಡ ತಾವರೆಯಾದಾಗ: ಚಿಕ್ಕಲ್ಲೂರು ಸಿದ್ದಪ್ಪಾಜಿ ಜಾತ್ರೆ
When the Ember Became a Lotus: The Chikkalluru Siddappaji Jatre

Series habba-jaatre, order 4, age 12+, era "c. 15th century to today", CC-BY-SA-4.0.

Spine: a smith's seventh son puts a live coal on a wandering guru's palm, sees it bloom into a lotus, and is promised he will be "a lone god under a lone tamarind tree". He becomes the first Neelagara, survives a molten ingot to beg iron from his own kin, and settles at Chikkalluru. Every full moon in January, the villages the guru named bring the bamboo, and lakhs of hands lift the fire with "ughe", so the lone god is never alone.

Structure: scenes 1-7 are legend (ಐತಿಹ್ಯ, after Hiriyanna's field-recorded text). Scenes 8-11 follow one fictional Neelagara family (Ajja, Gowri 12, her brother 8, parents) through the real 2026 programme (3-7 Jan). Scene 12 separates legend from record, covers the disputes and the law neutrally, and notes that the next date is not announced.
