# ಒಂದೇ ಆನೆ: ಇಮ್ಮಡಿ ವೀರಬಲ್ಲಾಳ (hoysala/veera-ballala-ii), outline

**Spine line.** Ballala is born the son of a vassal while the empire above him falls apart. He wins his
name by taking a fort that no one could take and handing it back to the king who lost it. Then, when the
Seuna king's host comes south boasting "who will come to grips with me?", he charges it on one elephant
at Soratur. That charge makes the Hoysalas their own masters. Forty years of war follow, and in old age
he lifts up a fallen Chola as he once lifted the Pandya. He dies with his crown on his son's head, and
his sworn friend Lakshma keeps his word to him in stone.

Five named people: ಬಲ್ಲಾಳ, ಉಮಾದೇವಿ, ಲಕ್ಷ್ಮ, ಭಿಲ್ಲಮ, ನರಸಿಂಹ. Years in prose: ೧೧೭೩, ೧೨೧೮, ೧೨೨೦. Age band 12+ (kept after review, on the Vishnuvardhana precedent).
Turning point: scene 9 (Soratur), confirmed in scene 10 (Lokkigundi and independence).
Greatness through an act: scene 4 (the restored Pandya), echoed in scene 11 (the restored Chola).
