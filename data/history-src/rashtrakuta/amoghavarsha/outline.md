## ಪೀಠಿಕೆ
The Deccan c. 800: the Rashtrakutas, a house with the Garuda on its seal, had taken the Chalukya
fortune and carried their horses to the Himalaya under Govinda III. Why this king matters: the
longest reign of the dynasty (more than sixty years), a capital he built, the oldest surviving
Kannada book written under his eye, a foreign merchant who ranked him with the Caliph and the
emperor of China, and a charter of his own that records a left finger given to a goddess for his
people. No face of him survives. Facts: f-rashtrakuta-context, f-names, f-no-portrait.

## ಸಾರಾಂಶ
Ten lines: a boy born in a war camp, crowned too young, nearly lost the kingdom, won it back with
loyal men, beat Vengi, built Manyakheta, ruled more than sixty years, was ranked by a foreign merchant
among the four great kings, bowed to a naked teacher, watched the first Kannada book on poetry made in
his name, gave a finger for his people, and kept walking away from the throne.
