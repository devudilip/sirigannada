# ಅಮೋಘವರ್ಷ ನೃಪತುಂಗ: outline (rewrite to the storytelling template, 2026-10-23)

Spine: Amoghavarsha, born in a war camp and crowned at fourteen, must become what the drums proclaimed on his crowning day, the lord who protects the humble; first the chiefs who broke their oaths stand in the way (and later his own son), then a sickness no army can stop; won back with the old commander Patalamalla and his cousin Karka, his story turns when he goes alone to the goddess, without crown, umbrella or army, and gives his left finger for his people; it costs a piece of his body, and later the kingdom itself, given away more than once; no one knows his face, but his own copper charter tells of the finger, and the oldest surviving Kannada book, which named the land from the Kaveri to the Godavari, carries his fame.

Same spine, names and order as the 2026-10-20 screenplay (history-video/rashtrakuta/amoghavarsha/screenplay.md). One addition the screenplay cut: the general's southern war and the son's revolt (scene 9), kept because the book must use the scene-07 picture and it is a second betrayal that echoes the first.
