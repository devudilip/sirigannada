# ಮೂರನೇ ಗೋವಿಂದ: outline

**Spine:** The younger son his father chose over his elder brother wins his throne against twelve kings, then wins them back with mercy, and carries the Rashtrakuta Garuda to the Himalaya, where two words, ಹೆದರಬೇಡಿ, become his greatness.
