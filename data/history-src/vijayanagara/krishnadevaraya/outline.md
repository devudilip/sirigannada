# ಕೊಟ್ಟ ಉಂಗುರ, ನಿಂತ ಕಂಚು: ಕೃಷ್ಣದೇವರಾಯ: outline (rewrite)

Spine: The prince who never asked for the throne, saved one night by his minister Timmarasu, sets out to win the three forts of an old testament; at Raichur the line breaks under Adil Shah's guns, he stands, sends his ring home as a token of his death, charges and wins, and then chooses mercy at the river. He is remembered in bronze at Tirumala with joined palms, not a sword.
