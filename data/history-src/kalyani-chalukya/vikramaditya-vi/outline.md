# ಆರನೇ ವಿಕ್ರಮಾದಿತ್ಯ: book outline (rewrite, 2026-10-23)

Spine: Vikramaditya, the second son who refused the throne because it was his elder brother's right, is driven from Kalyana by that brother's suspicion and carries his little brother Jayasimha out with him; when the elder brother Someshvara joins the enemy, the throne falls to him and he rubs out the Saka era; what stands in the way is the rebellion of the very brother he saved; it turns when his line bends and he charges on his own elephant, and at the forest's edge he lays his hand on his brother's shoulder instead of punishing him; it costs him father, elder brother and younger brother; today: forty years after him stones still counted the years in his name, and what is remembered is that hand.

Names in the prose (same as the screenplay): ವಿಕ್ರಮಾದಿತ್ಯ, ಸೋಮೇಶ್ವರ (the elder brother only; the father is never named), ಜಯಸಿಂಹ, ಬಿಲ್ಹಣ. Years in the prose: ೧೦೬೮, ೧೦೭೬, ೧೧೨೬.
